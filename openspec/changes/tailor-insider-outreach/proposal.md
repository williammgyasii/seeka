## Why

Today's drafts are generic. A message to a recruiter, a hiring manager, and a
team member who might refer the candidate should differ, and each should lead
with the candidate's real strengths for this job.

## What Changes

- Tailor email and LinkedIn drafts to the insider type: recruiter, hiring
  manager, or potential referrer.
- Cite résumé strengths that match the job, drawn only from confirmed facts.
- Keep the existing rules: no invented metrics, credentials, relationships, or
  contact facts; the user reviews every draft; LinkedIn drafts are copy-only;
  outreach is recorded manually.

## Depends on

- `add-insider-discovery` (insider type)
- Better with `add-resume-extraction` and `add-job-fit-verdict` (strengths)

## Open questions for the deep dive

- Whether drafts are template-based or AI-written, and how grounding is
  enforced if AI writes them.
- Whether to include an application or CV link in the message.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `job-seeker-workflow`: Honest contact and outreach drafts become specific to
  the insider type and cite strengths.

## Impact

- Outreach domain policy and draft UI.
